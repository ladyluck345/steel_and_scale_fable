#country
Home to almost every [[Lore/Species/Dragon\|Dragon]], including [[Lore/Species/Dragonkin\|Dragonkin]].
The landscape is scoured by "zones" of various geographical profiles -Volcanoes, desert, Coral reefs, Fields, Forests - all due to the dragons innate [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Elemental Magic\|Elemental Magic]] allowing the biomes to coexist.
