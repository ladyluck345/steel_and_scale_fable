#country 
The multicultural City State ruled by a democratically elected council that borders an area of the [[Lore/Wild Lands/Wild Lands\|Wild Lands]] known as the [[Amnesty/The Dark Forest/The Dark Forest\|The Dark Forest]]. Formed by several retired adventurers 300 years ago.

Founding Families include 
Fell - Aberrant Fey Carpenters led by Applejack Fell
Dale - Human Farmers and blacksmiths led by Alana Dale
Arach - Forest Dragonkin Textile Weavers led by the siblings Vayla and Marik Arach.
Smiths - Human and Fey cooks and hunters led by Grayson Smith
Ozmanthys - Cambion Healers and Magic led by Aster Ozmanthys
