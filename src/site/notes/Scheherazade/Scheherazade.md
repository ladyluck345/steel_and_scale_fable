A desert area that separates the City State of [[Amnesty/Amnesty\|Amnesty]] from the rest of the [[Nameless Lands/Nameless Lands\|Nameless Lands]]. It is home to disparate small kingdoms based around the limited resources.
It is also called the Badlands due to  centuries of warfare from remaining vassals and decedents from [[Lore/Lore Figures/Dracula\|Dracula]]s Horde.
