A desert area that separates the City State of [[Amnesty/Amnesty\|Amnesty]] from the rest of the [[Nameless Lands/Nameless Lands\|Nameless Lands]]. It is home to disparate villages and limited resources.
It is also called Scheherazade to several local populations though centuries  of warfare from numerous short lived kingdoms and then the agressive descendants from [[Lore/Lore Figures/Dracula\|Dracula]]s Horde lower it's reputation.
