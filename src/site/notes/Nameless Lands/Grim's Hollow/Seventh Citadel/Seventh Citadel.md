#namelessLands #GrimsHollow 
The Seventh Citadel id the iteration of the Citadel, which was re-founded after the city's rebellion 150 years ago against [[Lore/Lore Figures/Victor Frankenstein\|Victor Frankenstein]]. It is geared towards advancement for the betterment of civilization and the storage of unique items. After the third time  it was established to have a caretaker who would forsake their identity to simply become [[Nameless Lands/Grim's Hollow/Seventh Citadel/Management/The Curator\|The Curator]] and separate leadership structure to the [[Nameless Lands/Nameless Lands\|Nameless Lands]] and managed through unknown means.
First 3 fell to Conquest,1 past due to the region being sucked into another dimension for 2 years and 4 months. 1 changed due to disaster - an earthquake. One due to the Lich taking over and then when he was slain the seventh iteration.
The Citadel operates in ways unknown to those outside it much to the chagrin of [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
