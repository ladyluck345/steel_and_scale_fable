#namelessLands  #city  
Reestablished by a [[Lore/Lore Figures/Victor Frankenstein\|Victor Frankenstein]] who unified the families of the warring lords, it was liberated 150 years ago from his rule that quickly devolved into fascism and is known for its scientific advancements. Home to the [[Nameless Lands/Grim's Hollow/Seventh Citadel/Seventh Citadel\|Seventh Citadel]] and ruled by [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]]. The city has been rebuilt countless times and it’s difficult to place just how far back the [[Nameless Lands/Nameless Lands\|Nameless Lands]] was founded or even if this was the location of the original city.
