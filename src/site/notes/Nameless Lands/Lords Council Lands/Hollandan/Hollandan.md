 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
 A lord gained the ability to speak to animals and understand them after eating a snake spirit of knowledge by accident, their decdents have inherited this abilitiy.
 Known for their rich Aurichalcum deposits and coal alongside massive magical trees.
  The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Hollandan/Family/Taliesin Hollandan\|Taliesin Hollandan]].
