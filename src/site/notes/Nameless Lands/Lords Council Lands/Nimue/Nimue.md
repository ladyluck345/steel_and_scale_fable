 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
 This family was forever changed when the lord was killed by a village boy, whose family killed on the lords order, who was given a powerful enchanted blade given by a spirit from a lake. This blade is wielded by the family Head
 Much like House [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Carabas/Carabas\|Carabas]], they are the source of powerful order of knights, oil fields and reservoirs of water.
 The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Nimue/Family/Tomas Nimue\|Tomas Nimue]].
