 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
  Once subject to a curse on their family Heads heir, one that animated their long hair causing it to perform violent acts. They were cured by a wandering sorcerer in a cloak of thorns.
  They are known for advancements in medical science and have excellent agriculture. 
   The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Ivaldi/Family/Bryn Ivaldi\|Bryn Ivaldi]].
