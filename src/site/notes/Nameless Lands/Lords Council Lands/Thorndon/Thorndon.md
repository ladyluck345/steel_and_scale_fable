 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
 A foreign princess that married into the family was trapped in a tower surrounded by thorns by a [[Lore/Species/Court Fae\|Court Fae]]. They managed to save themself with a spinning wheel that eventually crafted a flying carpet so they could soar away.
 A thriving agriculture industry.
The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Thorndon/Family/Basile Thorndon\|Basile Thorndon]].
