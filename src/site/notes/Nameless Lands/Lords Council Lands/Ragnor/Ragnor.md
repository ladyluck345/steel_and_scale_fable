 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]]. The family was long ago a powerful clan from [[Útgarða/Útgarða\|Útgarða]] and have a strong connection to their culture alongside "newer" traditions.
 The only family with a nonhuman head and one of the youngest current family heads, [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Ragnor/Family/Fenrir Ragnor\|Fenrir Ragnor]].
 
 A excellent source of farmed animals, vast ore deposits and hydrothermal vents .
