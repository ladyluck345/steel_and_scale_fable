#namelessLands 
A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
The original domain of [[Lore/Lore Figures/Victor Frankenstein\|Victor Frankenstein]] and the first to turn on him. They are one of the leading forces in mutagenics, Pharmacology and industrial manufacturing.
The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Woolstencroft/Family/Percy Woolstencroft\|Percy Woolstencroft]].
