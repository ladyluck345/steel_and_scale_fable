#namelessLands 
A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
Known as one of the previous Lords was cursed to feel no fear.
The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Elworth/Family/Lysander Elworth\|Lysander Elworth]].
Male members born into the family have middle names tied to one of territories, which they usually end up running.
Whereas the female members have middle names tied to flowers.
Their estates include Joymont, Harcourt, Reavonrox and Kingston
Known for production of electronics, magical tools and efficent workers.
