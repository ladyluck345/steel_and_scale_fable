 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
 The house where the infamous [[Lore/Species/Court Fae\|Court Fae]] [[Lore/Lore Figures/Rumplestiltskin\|Rumplestiltskin]] ripped himself in half after he was beaten in a game of guessing where he tried to steal the Lord's baby.
 A large industry for fabric alongside rich mineral deposits of  iron and silver.
  The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Lindwell/Family/Eiman Lindwell\|Eiman Lindwell]].
