 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]]. 
 This house was saved from a bloody inheritence war when the true heir was found and raised to leadership by the actions of [[Lore/Lore Figures/Puss in Boots\|Puss in Boots]]. The cat asked for nothing more than boots and a place to rest.
 They are known for their excellent order of knights, production of leather and remarkable amount of grain production.
 
 The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Carabas/Family/Jeanne Carabas\|Jeanne Carabas]].
