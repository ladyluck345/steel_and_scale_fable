The governing body of the [[Nameless Lands/Nameless Lands\|Nameless Lands]]. 
There is a seat for every owner of a recognized landed territory. They are almost entirely Human men and only in rare cases are they not men and or [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]]. There was originally 102 lords which over centuries has whittled down to 13, with their various vassals underneath them making up the majority of the noble population.
