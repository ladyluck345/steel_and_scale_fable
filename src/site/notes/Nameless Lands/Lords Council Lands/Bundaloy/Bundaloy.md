#namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
 A family who lost in a whimisical game which resulted in the lords son being turned into a frog. They were only cured by a herb with leaves shaped like lips.
 A vast series of gold or copper mines and glass production.
The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Bundaloy/Gerard Bundaloy\|Gerard Bundaloy]].
