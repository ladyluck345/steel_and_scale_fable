 #namelessLands 
 A family that has one of the 13 main seats on [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]].
 A lord was killed by his bastard son using a cursed golden apple that caused great discord causing miraculous accident to kill them and freeing the land from his tyranny.
 They are known for their thriving paper industry and expert craftspeople. 
The current head of the family is [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/Vistil/Family/Paris Vistil\|Paris Vistil]].
