The [[Nameless Lands/Nameless Lands\|Nameless Lands]] are a series of territories controlled by [[Nameless Lands/Lords Council Lands/The Lords Council\|The Lords Council]]. The history was lost to time multiple times over leading to the nations name. A central unaligned city dwells in the middle - [[Nameless Lands/Grim's Hollow/Grim's Hollow\|Grim's Hollow]].
