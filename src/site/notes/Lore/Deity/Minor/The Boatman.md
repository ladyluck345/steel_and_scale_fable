Minor [[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of death and psychopomp to the Afterworlds. It is common practice to leave some form of currency on a loved one’s corpse for them to gain quicker passage.
Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Minor/The Boatman\|The Boatman]] are able to travel through gaps in [[Lore/Realm/Realm\|Realm]]s and hold sway over those who are dying or dead. They are also however unable to sleep though do not suffer exhaustion.

Edicts - Lay bodies to rest, Aid travelers and those who return from the dead, destroy unwilling or evil undead, Help others through painful changes.
Anathema - desecrate a corpse, take from the dead in bad faith, mock the grieving, neglect those in your care.
