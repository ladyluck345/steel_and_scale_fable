Minor [[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of Knowledge and The Moon.
Creator of the moon and collector of stories of the other gods creations - the earth and its inhabitants.

Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Minor/The Watcher\|The Watcher]] are able to learn rapidly as well as access [[Lore/Deity/Minor/The Watcher\|The Watcher]]s great library of knowledge. They are also however prone to bursts of hyper fixation and intense depression by proxy of understanding their place in the universe.

Edicts - Gather and share knowledge freely, observe the movements of the moon, planets, and stars; transcribe and learn new tales, chronicle events of the time you live in.
Anathema - destroy knowledge, disrespect the traditions of those around you, willfully spread ignorance or wrong information, trust in memory alone.
