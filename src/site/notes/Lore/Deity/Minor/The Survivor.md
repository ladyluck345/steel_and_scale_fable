Minor [[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of battle and conflict. 
They are said to be present on every battlefield and in every duel.
They mostly appear as a wounded warrior.

Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Minor/The Survivor\|The Survivor]] are able to quickly learn numerous ways of combat and strategy as well as survive great damage. They are also however known to experience greater pain and are known to scar easily.

Edicts - Offer your strength to aid others, sacrifice when others can’t, share training and tactics with allies

Anathema - Show continued mercy to the enemies of your people when such enemies prove they are undeserving, sacrifice others in your place, refuse a reasonable bet or duel, engage in mindless war.
