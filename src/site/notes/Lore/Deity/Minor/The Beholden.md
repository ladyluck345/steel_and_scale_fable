Minor [[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of Love and Art.
Their avatar looks different to every single entity and is behind the idea of Fated Mates.

Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Minor/The Beholden\|The Beholden]] are able to inspire love in others as well as change their forms. They are also however known to experience multiple forms of love much stronger.

Edicts - Comfort and encourage lovers, see the beauty in all things, express yourself through art and song , follow your passions.
Anathema - Destroy art or allow it to be destroyed, unless saving a life or pursuing greater art; engage in bigoted behavior, act without consent in pursuit of passion.
