[[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of the the Elements and creator of [[Lore/Species/Dragon\|Dragon]]s and by proxy [[Lore/Species/Dragonkin\|Dragonkin]].
They gave their blood to the world, giving it the elements.
They were distant from the rest of the gods due to their sense of superiority in their own creations and went into a state of sleep in [[Lore/Realm/The Underworlds\|The Underworlds]] after watching the fight between the forces of the Twin Gods create the [[God's Blood Archipelago/God's Blood Archipelago\|God's Blood Archipelago]].  

Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Scaled One\|The Scaled One]] are able to easily wield the elements and become more draconic - as such to become closer to their god.
They are also however said to become much larger and prone to vanity.


Edicts - Venerate scaled creatures, promote life and growth, respect and aid the flow of natural cycles, show pride in your accomplishments, seek to gather what you desire.
Anathema - disrespect sincere gifts, Deny a flying creature the ability to fly, ignore mistreatment of the environment.
