[[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of Light and the twin of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Adversary\|The Adversary]].
Creator of Angels and by proxy [[Lore/Species/Deva\|Deva]].
They rule the realm of Heaven and gave the world light along with the day.

Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Heavenly Host\|The Heavenly Host]] are able to control light, emotions of associated with the Seven Heavenly Virtues and the holy water of [[Lore/Realm/Heaven/Heaven\|Heaven]].
They are also however said to be prone to lessened emotions and tend to become blind.

Edicts - Strive for perfection in mind and body, seek and allow redemption, cast light in places of darkness.
Anathema - deny a repentant creature an opportunity for redemption, fail to strike down evil, languish in the darkness for no reason.
