A trio of [[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] sisters that oversee fate and created [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]]s.
They gave the first peoples souls, minds and the ability to change their fates.
They include [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Weavers/The Maiden\|The Maiden]], [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Weavers/The Mother\|The Mother]] and [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Weavers/The Crone\|The Crone]].
Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Weavers/The Weavers\|The Weavers]] are able to see visions of past present and future events though fluctuate in age.

Edicts - offer advice and guidance, provide comfort to the elderly, assist childbirths.
Anathema - disrespect mothers, violate hospitality once offered, force aid on those who do not want it, place your fate in chance without applying any effort
