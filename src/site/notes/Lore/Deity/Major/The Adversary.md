[[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of Darkness and the twin of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Heavenly Host\|The Heavenly Host]].
Creator of fiends and by proxy [[Lore/Species/Cambion\|Cambion]].
They rule the realm of Hell and gave the world darkness along with the night.

Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Adversary\|The Adversary]] are able to control shadows, emotion associated with one or more of the Deadly Sins as well as the the flames of [[Lore/Realm/Hell/Hell\|Hell]]. They are said however to be prone to strong emotions and tend to become mute the longer they live.

Edicts - Indulge your basest desires, wield cunning and weaknesses as your weapons , avenge all insults , work in shadows.
Anathema - Allow a slight to go unanswered, Break a contract you made, Fear the dark, deny your appetites.
