[[Lore/Deity/Deity\|Deity]] of Nature and creator of the [[Lore/Realm/Faerie/Fae/High Fae/High Fae\|High Fae]], [[Lore/Species/Wild Fae\|Wild Fae]] and by proxy [[Lore/Species/Aberrant Fae\|Aberrant Fae]].
They breathed life into the world to bring about nature.
They split off from the world and rule the realm of Faerie, also known as the Spirit Wilds.

Chosen of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Green Man\|The Green Man]] are said to be greatly radiate growth in both flora, leading to quicker maturation or titanic size, and fauna, leading to increased populations of animals that are enhanced in some way or another. They are also however said to slowly turn to wood or more animalistic.

Edicts - Preserve areas of natural wilderness, guide civilization to grow in harmony with nature, live sustainably and according to natural cycles
Anathema - damage natural environments, encourage imbalance in nature, break your word.
