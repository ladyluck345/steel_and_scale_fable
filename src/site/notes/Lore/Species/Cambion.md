#Species


A strain of [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]]s, they are mortals with [[Lore/Realm/Hell/Fiend/Fiend\|Fiend]]ish Heritage or the result of a demonic deal. They feel emotions strongly which can result in the stereotype of them being aggressive along with their connection to [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Adversary\|The Adversary]]. They have strong affinity towards the magic of darkness but are vulnerable to powers of light.
