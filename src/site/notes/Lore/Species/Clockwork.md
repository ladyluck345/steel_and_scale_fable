#Species 
Artificial beings of which a majority were created first 202 years ago by the 6th Citadel under the direction of [[Lore/Lore Figures/Victor Frankenstein\|Victor Frankenstein]] and the previous Caretaker [[Lore/Lore Figures/Dorian Grey\|Dorian Grey]]. Originally used for the Liches Armed Forces (LAF) and servants. Deactivated after the fall of Frankenstein 150 years ago, they were reworked 55 years ago during in the [[Nameless Lands/Grim's Hollow/Seventh Citadel/Seventh Citadel\|Seventh Citadel]] to rebuild sections of Lord territory and supplement the army to mixed reception. It was not until that they were “discovered” to be sentient and emotionally complex that they were given basic rights 30 years ago.

While most of the clockwork were built using prebuilt chassis, several specialized chassis were made including :
Talos - The main model used for the LAF that had inbuilt weapons and enhanced optics
Daedalus - Heavy construction and labor .
Galatea - High-class and exclusive servants to the elite. Few were ever made.
