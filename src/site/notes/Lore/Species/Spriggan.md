#Species 
A type of mortal [[Lore/Species/Aberrant Fae\|Aberrant Fae]] born of a [[Lore/Species/Wild Fae\|Wild Fae]] Dryad or of a pregnancy saturated with nature magic.
 They are usually the offspring of a [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]] and a dryad or some other fey spirit, though these unions happen rarely due to the lack of interaction between the two species. They can also occur when a child of [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]] parents is born in an area particularly touched by life magic.
