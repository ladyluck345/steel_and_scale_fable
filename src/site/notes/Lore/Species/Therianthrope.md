#Species 
Commonly known as a Lycan or Were.
A subtype of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Shapeshifter\|Shapeshifter]]. Beings (usually [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]]s or [[Lore/Species/Elf-Blood\|Elf-Blood]]) who can turn into wolves (or other animal) of a monstrous size possibly even an enhanced warrior form mixing humanoid and animal. Much is not known as they keep to themselves due to a history of persecution.

They are thought to become able to shift either very young or when coming of age depending on the process.
