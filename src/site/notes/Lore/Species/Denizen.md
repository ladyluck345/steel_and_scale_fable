#Species 
Beings formed from the [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Arcane Magic/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] that permeated the planes but none more so than in [[Lore/Realm/The Otherworlds/The Otherworld\|The Otherworld]], The plane that arcane magic originates when the bloods of gods and eldritch beings were spilt. Due to the nature of their home plane they function by law and order as both a way of life and survival mechanism. They are almost universally well versed in numerous forms of law and while they can reproduce asexually, they can have biological children if for some reason desired.
