#Species 
A blanket term for groups of [[Lore/Species/Aberrant Fae\|Aberrant Fae]] that are not immortal, mostly born from relations between themselves and other Elf-bloods or an Aberrant Fae and a [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]].
They mostly have animal-like features that vary from person to person based on genetics and environmental factors.
