#Species 
[[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]]oids shaped by draconic features that tie to the natural elements. They are a proud people not unlike the [[Lore/Species/Dragon\|Dragon]]s whose blood they house. They are looked on more fondly by dragons than any other race and are called by a word that roughly translates to Small Relative/Cousin.

There are several ways for a dragon folk to exist - 
The obvious but rarer option is a dragon mating with a non-dragon.

The more likely options include 
A [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]] having a child with a dragon folk as the draconic magic while affect the human soul in the womb.
A [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]] or their ancestor being blessed by a powerful dragons magic.
