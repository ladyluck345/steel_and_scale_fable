#Species 
A subtype of [[Lore/Realm/Faerie/Fae/High Fae/High Fae\|High Fae]]
“Monstrous and Animalistic”, 
These beings are wildly varied but have appearances that associate with the animalistic or natural.
Common subtypes include
Harpy, a fae with wings and avian features.
Bugbear, a fae with a beastly body with ursine features and enhanced strength.
Merfolk, a fae that live underwater most of the time. 
Dryad, a fae tied to a specific type of flora.
