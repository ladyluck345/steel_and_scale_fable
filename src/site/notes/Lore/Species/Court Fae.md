#Species 
A subspecies of [[Lore/Realm/Faerie/Fae/High Fae/High Fae\|High Fae]].
Elves capable of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Glamour\|Glamour]], a magic unique to them, and have strong magical ties to a special [[Lore/Wild Lands/Wild Lands\|Wild Lands]] called a Court.
