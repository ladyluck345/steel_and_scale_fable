#Species 
Beings created by and formed in the image of [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Scaled One\|The Scaled One]].
They are reptilian creatures that can innately connect to the elements.
Some are able to fly, burrow and even swim depending on their elemental connection - Earth, Fire, Forest, Ice, Storm, Water and even the rare Arcane.
They almost exclusively live in the nation of [[Scalehold/Scalehold\|Scalehold]].
