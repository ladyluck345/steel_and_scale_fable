#Species
One of the rarest strains of [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]]s. They are mortals with [[Lore/Realm/Heaven/Angels/Angel\|Angel]]ic heritage or were in an area after a “Miracle”. They are usually taken in by [[Lore/Deity/Major/The Heavenly Host\|The Heavenly Host]] church or are given very high expectations from their communities. They have strong affinity towards the magic of light but are vulnerable to powers of darkness.
