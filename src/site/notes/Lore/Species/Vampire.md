#Species 
A sub-type of [[Lore/Species/Revenant\|Revenant]] that feeds on blood and are weakened in direct sunlight.
They are functionally immortal when well fed with a varying level of abilities usually based on their age. Their eyes appear red and though they can turn others, the chances of success are highly situational.
