#GrimsHollow #NPC 
Species: [[Lore/Species/Human\|Human]]
Age: OLD (Deceased)
Charismatic Cult Leader and ‘Lich’ of [[Nameless Lands/Grim's Hollow/Grim's Hollow\|Grim's Hollow]]. Though they were described as a lich due to their seeming inability to die (mostly staged assassination attempts), they had no magical abilities what so ever. Came to great success by quickly rising to be in control of warring government and recruiting scientists from the [[Nameless Lands/Grim's Hollow/Frankenstein Era/Sixth Citadel\|Sixth Citadel]] to build a mechanical army, The [[Lore/Species/Clockwork\|Clockwork]], to keep his power. He was killed by unknown means around 150 years ago when his reign came to an end -  by the uprising or a papercut are the two major theories.
