They are creatures that embody any number of phenomena like locations, elements or cosmic forces.
They are found in almost every [[Lore/Realm/Realm\|Realm]] like Light Spirits in [[Lore/Realm/Heaven/Heaven\|Heaven]] or Shadow Spirits in [[Lore/Realm/Hell/Hell\|Hell]] though a vast amount originate from [[Lore/Realm/Faerie/Faerie\|Faerie]] which then emigrate to other planes like Fable.
