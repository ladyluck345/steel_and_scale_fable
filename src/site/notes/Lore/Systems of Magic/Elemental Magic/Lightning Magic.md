Lightning Magic is a branch of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Air Magic\|Air Magic]]. This magic allows a user to control thunder and lightning.

You have Advance to Casting rolls for Lightning Magic against entities which are
made of metal, or wearing metal armor. Metal naturally conducts electricity. If
you use a Lightning magic ability on a metal object, it will also affect all entities within a Short (6) range which are touching that metal object, or who have a conductive path to it. This also works on water, though you cannot do this if you are touching the water. You cannot use Lightning Magic underwater.
