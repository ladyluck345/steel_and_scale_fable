Metal Magic is a branch of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Earth Magic\|Earth Magic]], it extends a users control over earth into a
control over metallic materials as well.
In order for a user to use Metal magic, there must be at least some kind of metal object in range to use.
If there is limited metal around, such as just a few shards, then your Energy Limit
per round is reduced by 1.
If there is plenty of metal around you, or you are carrying a large amount of
metal, then your Energy Limit per round is increased by 1.
