Frost Magic is a branch of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Water Magic\|Water Magic]], which allows you to control Ice and cold.

Like Water Magic, a Frost Magic user cannot use this branch of magic if there is
not enough water around to be effective.
Frost Magic users are also more powerful in the cold, and less powerful in the heat. In warm or dry conditions, a users Energy limit per round is reduced by 1. 
In particularly cold conditions, assuming there is sufficient moisture nearby, a users Energy limit per round is increased by 1.
