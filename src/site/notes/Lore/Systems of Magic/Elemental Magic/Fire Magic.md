This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Elemental Magic\|Elemental Magic]] allows a user to control heat and flames. 

In cold, damp, or wet conditions, a users Energy limit per round is reduced by 1.
In particularly warm conditions, a users Energy limit per round is increased by 1.
A user cannot use Fire Magic underwater.
