This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Elemental Magic\|Elemental Magic]] allows a user to control stone, and other earthen materials.

In order for a user to use Earth magic, there must be at least enough stone, sand, dirt, or rubble nearby to be effective.
If there is limited earthen material around, such as just a few pebbles or
bricks, then the users Energy limit per round is reduced by 1.
If the terrain around the user has an abundance of unworked, natural stone, then
the users Energy limit per round is increased by 1.
