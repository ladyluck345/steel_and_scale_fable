The function of Elemental magic connects you the the world's ambient magic.
Elemental Magic offers control over the elements; Air, Earth, Fire, Water, and their related subtypes. In an area with the necessary elements available, this magic can be highly versatile and powerful, but can be deprived of that power easily by a change of environment.
