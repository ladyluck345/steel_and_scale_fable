Ash Magic is a branch of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Fire Magic\|Fire Magic]], allowing the control of hot ash, smoke, and
cinders.
A user cannot use Ash Magic underwater.
A user require burning material to use your magic. If there is no flammable
material available, then you cannot use Ash Magic.
In damp, or wet conditions, a users Energy limit per round for magic is reduced
by 1.
If a user is near a fire, smoke, or an abundance of flammable material, a users
Energy limit per round for magic is increased by 1.
