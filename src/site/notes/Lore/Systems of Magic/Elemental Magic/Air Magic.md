This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Elemental Magic\|Elemental Magic]] allows a user to control the winds.

When you use Air Magic on a willing entity (including yourself), you can choose to
move them harmlessly, not inflicting any damage or conditions.
You cannot use Air magic without any air present in your surroundings (e.g. underwater).
