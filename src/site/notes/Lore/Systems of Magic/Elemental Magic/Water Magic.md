This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Elemental Magic\|Elemental Magic]] allows a user to control water, and other water-based liquids.

In order for a user to use water magic, there must be at least enough water or moisture nearby to be effective.
If there is limited water around, the users Energy limit per round is reduced by 1.
If a user is close to a large body of water, or if it is raining, the users Energy limit per turn is increased by 1.
