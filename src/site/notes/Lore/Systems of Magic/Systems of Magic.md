Magic is most commonly the ability for the soul to connect to different sources.
[[Lore/Systems of Magic/Arcane Magic/Arcane Magic\|Arcane Magic]] connects the user to the foreign pool of magic in the Otherworld.
[[Lore/Systems of Magic/Divine Magic/Divine Magic\|Divine Magic]] connects the user to the power of the gods and supernatural forces.
[[Lore/Systems of Magic/Elemental Magic/Elemental Magic\|Elemental Magic]] connects the user to the world's ambient magic.
[[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Forbidden Magic\|Forbidden Magic]] allows the user to directly affect the soul.

There are different magic like effects that are mostly attributed to forbidden magic like [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Witch\|Witch]]es, [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Glamour\|Glamour]] and [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Shapeshifter\|Shapeshifter]]s.
