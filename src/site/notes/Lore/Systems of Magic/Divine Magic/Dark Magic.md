This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Divine Magic/Divine Magic\|Divine Magic]] gives you a connection to darkness and shadows.  This includes the passive ability to see in dim light.

In Bright Light, a user cannot cast your Dark magic.
In Standard Light, a users Energy Limit per round for magic is reduced by 1.
In the Dark, a users Energy Limit per round for magic is increased by 1.
