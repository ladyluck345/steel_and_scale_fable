This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Divine Magic/Divine Magic\|Divine Magic]] gives a connection to the spirit realm, and the dead.
This includes the passive ability to see ghosts and other spirits, and may be able to communicate with them. It also makes mindless undead less hostile to you.

If there is a recently deceased body (died within the last day) within a Short distance (6 spaces) of you, a user may “expend it” to use as 1 Energy point towards the casting of a Death Magic ability. This can take a user above the maximum energy limit per round. A body cannot be used this way more than once.
