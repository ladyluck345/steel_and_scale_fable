This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Divine Magic/Divine Magic\|Divine Magic]] gives you a connection to summon and control light in its most holy form, most often connected to the Heavenly Host.

If a user in the Dark, they cannot cast any of your Light abilities.
In Dim Light, a users Energy limit per round for magic is reduced by 1.
In Bright light, a users Energy limit per round for magic is increased by 1.
Light provided by the Illumination ability does not affect these limitations.
