The function of Divine Magic connects you to the power of the gods and supernatural forces which are often in opposition, but exist in cosmic balance.
Divine Magic is the manifestation of the power of the gods and cosmic forces, 
These divine forces are not inherently good or evil; all are necessary, though individual adherents may not understand this.
