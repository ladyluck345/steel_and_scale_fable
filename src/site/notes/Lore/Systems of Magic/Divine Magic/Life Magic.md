This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Divine Magic/Life Magic\|Life Magic]] gives you a connection to the natural world of flora and fauna, which you are able to call on for aid. This includes the passive ability to small natural plants grow, such as flowers or berries. 

The following properties only apply to abilities that rely on manipulating plant life. If there are no plants, or even arable soil, nearby or accessible, then you cannot use any of your relevant abilities.
If a user is in an urban area, a desert, or any other area which has limited access to plant life, your Energy limit per round for magic is reduced by 1 If you are in a very wild area, with many plants and animals nearby, your Energy limit per round for magic is increased by 1.
