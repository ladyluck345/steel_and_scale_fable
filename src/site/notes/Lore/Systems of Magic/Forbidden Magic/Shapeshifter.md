Through a mix of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Forbidden Magic\|Forbidden Magic]] and [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Divine Magic/Life Magic\|Life Magic]], users can shift into different beasts. They usually stick to a particular animal form though through training can gain additional forms or even augment them.
