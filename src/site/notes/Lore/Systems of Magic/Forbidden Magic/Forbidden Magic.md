The function of Forbidden Magic allows you to connect directly to the soul and as such allows for control over the mind and body. Forbidden Magic is taboo in many societies, due to the way it insidiously interacts with living creatures, and can be incredibly harmful. However it also has many positive uses. Forbidden Magic is often used by esoteric occultists, woodland shamans, and travelling mystics, magic users who exist on the fringes of society, or are able to hide the nature of their magical practices.
