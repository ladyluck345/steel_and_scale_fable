This type of [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Forbidden Magic\|Forbidden Magic]] allows your soul to connect to minds and see the unseen.
The passive form of this ability allows the user to seem more affable to those of a lower resolve and communicate small telepathic messages towards other people.
