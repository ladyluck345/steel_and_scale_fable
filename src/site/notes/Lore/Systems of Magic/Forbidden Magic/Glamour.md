A unique magic tied to [[Lore/Species/Court Fae\|Court Fae]] that can create illusions or enchant others. It is considered a [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Forbidden Magic\|Forbidden Magic]] due to its ability to draw raw magic from a [[Lore/Wild Lands/Wild Lands\|Wild Lands]] into the users soul.
