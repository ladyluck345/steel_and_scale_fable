A person who has bound their soul to a [[Lore/Species/Familiar\|Familiar]] as a means of prolonging the lives of both and sharing magic through a [[Lore/Systems of Magic/Forbidden Magic/Forbidden Magic\|Forbidden Magic]] soul-binding ritual. They usually gather in groups no smaller than 3 and are either beloved by the community or mistrusted due to their souls being split.  They can have any form of magic but can gain new abilities or heightned power from the connection to their familiar
